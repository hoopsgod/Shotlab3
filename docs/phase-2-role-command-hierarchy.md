# Phase 2 — Role Command Hierarchy

## Objective

Turn the Coach and Player home screens into decision-first daily briefs without changing routes, permissions, persistence, or demo/registered behavior.

## Product hierarchy

1. One primary decision owns the strongest dark or team-accent treatment.
2. Evidence explains the decision without becoming a second call to action.
3. Coach guidance and queued work follow in editorial order.
4. Desktop insight rails show one priority card; supporting intelligence uses quiet light surfaces.
5. Activation remains visible, but cannot compete visually with the Coach hero.

## Copy standard

- Prefer a specific action or outcome over system language.
- Use natural singular and plural forms.
- Do not label locally logged data as verified.
- Keep headings scannable and supporting sentences to one decision-relevant idea.

## Guardrails

- Preserve the Phase 1 demo/registered parity contract.
- Keep all current navigation targets and actions.
- Maintain 44px minimum interactive targets.
- Preserve reduced-motion behavior and mobile-safe geometry.
- Certify the production bundle and exact deployed head before closure.
