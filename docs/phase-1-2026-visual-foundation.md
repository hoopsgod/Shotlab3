# Phase 1: 2026 Visual Foundation

Phase 1 replaces ShotLab's competing visual treatments with one cross-role product system.

## Product direction

ShotLab uses a light-first performance operating system:

- warm cream application canvas
- white operational surfaces
- deep ink typography
- one deliberate navy performance hero per home experience
- lime reserved for primary actions, progress, and selected navigation
- independent semantic colors for success, information, warning, and danger

## Canonical geometry

- small radius: 12px
- standard radius: 18px
- hero radius: 26px
- spacing follows a 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 scale
- shadows are restrained and communicate elevation rather than decoration

## Typography and iconography

- system-native SF-style display and text stacks
- sentence case for actions and headings unless a compact semantic label requires uppercase
- one consistent thin rounded icon weight
- no imported decorative font is required by the new visual foundation

## Surface hierarchy

1. canvas
2. operational paper surface
3. inset mineral surface
4. dark performance hero
5. transient glass navigation or sheet

Glass is limited to navigation and transient controls. It is not used as a general card material.

## Role parity

Coach and Player experiences share:

- canvas and surface colors
- typography
- spacing
- radii
- button geometry
- mobile navigation
- empty, loading, and recovery behavior

Role-specific content remains distinct, but it must feel like one product.

## Compatibility

Phase 1 does not change authentication logic, routes, permissions, persistence, scoring, database schemas, demo availability, or team-data behavior.

## Visual acceptance

Before merge, fixed mobile viewport screenshots must be reviewed for:

- sign-in
- Coach Demo home
- Player Demo home

The phase is not complete if the screenshots show competing visual systems, excessive glow, inconsistent radii, cramped touch targets, unreadable team colors, or more than one visually dominant action in the initial viewport.
