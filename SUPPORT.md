## Account Help

If you need help signing in, accessing the correct player or coach account, or resolving an account setup issue, contact support with:

- Your name.
- The email address used for your ShotLab account.
- Whether you are a player, coach, or reviewer.
- A brief description of the issue.
- Screenshots if they help explain the problem. Do not include passwords or sensitive payment information.

## Delete Account / Data Requests

To request deletion of your account or associated ShotLab data, email support with:

- The subject line: `Delete my ShotLab account/data`.
- The email address used for your ShotLab account.
- Whether you want your account deleted, your data deleted, or both.

Support will review the request and provide confirmation or next steps. Some records may be retained if required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational reasons.
